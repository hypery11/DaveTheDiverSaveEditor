Support DiveSaveEd

DiveSaveEd is free and MIT-licensed. No ads, no paid version, nothing locked behind a payment — and that isn't changing.

Who you'd be sending it to

The donation page is labelled fsd — that's just the handle on the payment page, not a company or a person you need to recognise. It won't say “DiveSaveEd” anywhere, so it's worth knowing that before you get there rather than wondering once you do.

Read this before you click

The donation page takes crypto only. You need a wallet you already have funds in — there is no card, Apple Pay, PayPal or bank option, and no amount field. If that isn't something you already use, please don't go out of your way.

What it pays for

Keeping the item and fish reference database current when the game patches, and the Apple Developer membership (US$99/year) that lets the app be signed and notarized — which is why macOS opens it without a scary warning.

Worth more than money

Genuinely. Any of these helps this project more than a few dollars:

  • Report a bug — the app's Help menu fills in the details for you.
  • Fix a translation. The app ships in four languages and I only speak two of them well.
  • Tell someone who plays on a Mac.
  • Star the repo, so other people find it.

The donation page

Takes USDC or ETH on Base, Ethereum, BSC, Arbitrum, Polygon, Gnosis and Optimism. Behind the secondary button it also takes BTC, USDT on Tron, and Solana. USDC on Base is the cheapest.
